Questions, answered

How do I breathe with my face under water?

The breathing attachment clips to the rim and sits in the basin, so you can breathe slowly and comfortably while your whole face is submerged. Use the included nose clip for the best experience.

How quickly will I see less puffiness?

Customers often notice their skin looks tighter and less puffy once their face warms back up after a session. Consistency matters more than duration.

How long should I stay under?

Start with 30 seconds and listen to your body. Build up gradually to a maximum of 2 minutes.

Can I use warm water or skincare in it?

Yes. Beyond ice‑water facials, you can create warm, soothing soaks for relaxation and skin hydration.

Is it safe for everyone?

The FaceTub is safe for most people. If you have preexisting conditions, respiratory issues or extreme cold sensitivity, consult your doctor before use.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. The tub has a lifetime warranty. If it’s ever damaged, we’ll send you a replacement.

How do I clean it?

Rinse with water after each use. For a deeper clean, use a mild soap, then wipe or air dry and store somewhere cool and dry.
